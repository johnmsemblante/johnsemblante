# Content review — 8 November 2026

## Focus and findings

The primary homepage search phrase is **Cebu Web Developer**. The previous homepage title emphasized the professional role, while several section headings used general slogans. Existing career history, location, services and project examples provide more specific material for visitors deciding whether to contact John.

## Revisions

- Homepage title/H1 and default description now state the Cebu web development focus. The full name remains visible, and the professional role stays WordPress/Full Stack Developer.
- Profile copy describes the supplied career start in 2013 at Cebu Rent A Car, Mandaue City location, WordPress work, tools and practical website problems.
- Service and workflow text explains the inputs, tasks and review process in first-person language.
- About, Work and Contact copy has a distinct purpose: career background, public project evidence, and inquiry guidance. Contact also welcomes remote roles without promising immediate availability.
- Blog listing introductions match between initial HTML and live rendering. The Ventures directory introduces the personal businesses without forcing a development keyword into their customer-facing offers.
- README and the SEO guide document the keyword placement and editing locations.

## Scope and evidence

This is an editorial revision based on the checked-in profile, supplied career history and existing project/venture content. It is not independent verification of employment status or client outcomes, keyword search-volume research, or a promise of rankings. Historical project records, employer dates, managed article bodies and individual venture offers retain their existing facts. No invented testimonials, results, certifications or new business locations were added.

Google's guidance favors helpful, reliable content written for people. AI assistance alone does not make content ineligible; rewriting cannot guarantee an AI-detector result. Avoid repeated keyword lists, artificial local landing pages and unsupported claims. Future improvements should add real project contributions and first-hand troubleshooting examples.

References: [People-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [AI content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/02/google-search-and-ai-content).
